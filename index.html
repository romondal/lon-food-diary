Lón food diary

A diary for logging food in Ireland that feels like a paper notebook, with Dunnes Stores, Tesco Ireland and SuperValu products built in. Everything in the phone works. Use these links to jump to a flow.

Flows

Camera, barcode and voice input are simulated because the prototype can't use device hardware. Product values are sample data based on typical Irish pack labels and need checking against real packs before production use. Product photos are real pack shots from Open Food Facts contributors (CC BY-SA 3.0). Produce and the breakfast plate are from Wikimedia Commons, each credited on its page.
